SLI vs SLO

The SLI is the ruler. The SLO is the mark. 99.7% succeed is a reading. It is a miss only after you promised 99.9%.

Latency percentiles

p50 stays near 50 ms. p99 spikes to 10 s. An average of the same window looks unchanged.

Mean vs median

Walk 100 requests with a 1 s timeout. Mean leaves the median. The chopped tail creates false outliers and hides real hangs.

Same SLO, two shapes

Both services pass 99% under 100 ms. Only one feels instant. One target cannot pin the shape of the curve.

Two workload classes

Same Set RPC. A pipeline can wait 1 s. A small click cannot. One number cannot serve both.

Error budget

99.9% succeed means 1 of 1,000 may fail. That leftover is the spend. A 100% SLO sets it to 0.

Choosing targets

Do not copy tonight's 10 ms if heroes produce it. Keep a line as an SLO only if people use it to decide ship or wait.

Control loop

Measure, compare, decide, act. Same 28 ms to 45 ms rise is a fire at 50 ms and a shrug at 200 ms.
