CONCEPT What's a reverse proxy?

A normal proxy sits in front of clients and hides them from the internet. A reverse proxy does the opposite: it sits in front of servers and hides them from clients.

The GFE is Google's reverse proxy. Your browser's TCP connection ends at the GFE — it never reaches the Shakespeare frontend, backend, or Bigtable directly.

Without one

Browser needs the real IP of a live backend, handles TLS itself, and breaks if that exact server goes down.

With the GFE

Browser only ever knows one address. GFE handles TLS, picks a healthy backend via GSLB, and can swap servers freely.
